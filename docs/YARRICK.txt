COMMISSAR SEBASTIAN YARRICK — PENAL LEGION

Yarrick belongs to the existing Penal Legion mod, Workshop ID 2525618649,
inside the Eternal War Foundry library. He has no separate mod identity,
SteamID, distribution archive or load-order entry.

Recruit from the existing Astra Militarum hero building. The normal Lord
Commissar and all prior hero recruitment entries remain available.

Weapons: existing bolt pistol, custom red power claw using Power Klaw rules,
and Bale Eye using the complete standard Lasgun definition. The eye has a
dedicated animated beam socket; the claw follows the original left hand.

Old Man of Armageddon starts trained and restores 9 morale at rank one,
matching vanilla Summary Execution's maximum. Further ranks restore 12/15.
No allied soldier is executed. Radius 3; cooldown 3.

Targeted Basilisk Strike resolves two standard Earthshaker shells against one
enemy within 6 tiles. Accuracy 6, damage 8, penetration 4, Large Blast,
Barrage and Ordnance. Cooldown 5. Personal hero damage bonuses do not apply.

Call of Armageddon is ONE trainable skill with FIVE purchased ranks.
It starts untrained. Hero experience levels do not automatically unlock calls.
  Rank 1: one fresh Guardsmen squad.
  Rank 2: one fresh Guardsmen squad plus one fresh Leman Russ.
  Rank 3: both start with 1 extra experience level.
  Rank 4: both start with 2 extra experience levels.
  Rank 5: both start with 3 extra experience levels.
Experience increments preserve the game's difficulty bonuses.
Both placements must be on empty land within 2 tiles of Yarrick. From rank 2,
the same command changes to a free tank-placement completion step after the
squad is placed. This step cannot be purchased or independently initiate a
call. Only one Call of Armageddon command is eligible at a time. Cooldown 10
belongs to the single purchased skill and persists through skill upgrades.
Tank experience is reserved when the call starts and does not change if the
skill is upgraded before tank placement.
Reinforcements are permanent and pay normal upkeep.

This Ends Here targets one ally within 3 tiles, adding +100% base damage for
2 rounds. Cooldown 5. Other percentage damage bonuses are not compounded.

VALIDATION
Rules, reference integrity, mesh weights/bones and 14 native animation clips
pass offline checks. Independent native import and render pass. The beam
socket follows the head within 0.0001 model units in six animation types.
The five purchased ranks are checked independently of hero levels 1-10,
including untrained heroes, blocked placements, cooldowns and serialized
pending reservations. These are XML model tests, not live game acceptance.
Live combat and save/load validation are pending; do not claim game acceptance.

Recruit Yarrick through the existing hero building. Verify 9-morale rally,
pistol/claw/eye attacks, two artillery resolutions, and This Ends Here's
two-round duration and five-round cooldown. Check all five purchased skill

Verify no additional Call of Armageddon skills appear in the level-up menu.

the action (the temporary rank-routing trait must be removed synchronously).
Save/reload with both a pending tank and an active damage buff.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
The vanilla action rank range is -1 (untrained) through 4 (rank five).
A temporary rankMax=4 trait inherits the source action rank and clamps
movementMax to 0..4 solely during synchronous effect routing. Numeric
movementMax conditions select the exact experience and armour reservation
steps. Separate ordered areas attach, route and remove this trait. No hero
level conditions, background companion or executable modifications are used.
The level effect uses the same native mechanism as Forbidden Knowledge.
Its increments avoid rounded addUnit interpolation for the 0/0/1/2/3 curve.
The four internal tank completions are non-levelable (rank=rankMax=0),
mutually exclusive, and share the main skill's visible name.
